Delete your account.

To permanently delete your account and all associated data, follow the steps below inside the app.

1
Open the LUUP. app and make sure you are logged in.
2
Go to your Profile tab.
3
Tap Settings.
4
Tap Delete Account and confirm your choice.

Your account and all associated personal data — including your name, email address, profile, and location history — will be permanently deleted within 24 hours. This action cannot be undone.
