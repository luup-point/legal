Common questions
How do I share my location with friends?
+
Open the map, go to the Friends tab and tap the location icon. You'll be prompted to allow location access — this only runs while sharing is active. You can turn it off at any time.
My friends can't see me on the map.
+
Make sure live sharing is turned on and that you've granted location permission set to "Always Allow" in your phone settings. If the issue persists, try toggling sharing off and back on.
How do I add friends on LUUP.?
+
Go to the Friends tab and allow LUUP. to access your contacts. Friends who already have the app will appear automatically. You can also invite them directly from the app.
How do I delete my account?
+
Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. Your data will be permanently removed within 24 hours. This action cannot be undone.
LUUP. is not available in my country.
+
LUUP. is currently launching in Portugal. We're expanding to more countries soon — follow us for updates.
