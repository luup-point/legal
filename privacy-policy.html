LUUP.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 June 2026

1. Who We Are

LUUP. is a festival experience application operated by Diogo Pinho, located at R Caetano Maria Batalha 3, 7D, 2800-040 Almada, Portugal. For any privacy-related queries, contact us at support@luuppoint.com.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Data you provide directly

  • Full name or display name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Date of birth (optional)
  • Gender (optional)
  • Profile picture (optional)

2.2 Data collected automatically

  • Precise GPS location (foreground and background, with your permission)
  • Device information (model, operating system, app version)
  • Usage data (session duration, app opens, feature interactions)
  • Crash and performance data
  • Anonymised, aggregated location data derived from your GPS coordinates, used to generate crowd density analytics (heatmaps). This data is irreversibly stripped of all personal identifiers before use.

2.3 Data from third-party sign-in

If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email address from those providers. We do not receive your password.

2.4 Device permissions

We request the following permissions:

  • Precise location (continuous) — to share your live location with friends during events
  • Precise location (non-continuous) — to show your position on the festival map
  • Background location — to maintain location sharing when the app is not in the foreground
  • Contacts — to help you find friends already using LUUP. Phone numbers are hashed before any comparison; raw numbers never leave your device
  • Camera — to set your profile picture
  • Photo library — to select a profile picture from your device
  • Motion sensors — to detect whether you are moving or stationary, used to optimise location update frequency

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Create and manage your account
  • Display your location to friends you have accepted on LUUP.
  • Help you find friends from your contacts who are already on LUUP.
  • Send push notifications about events, friend requests, and activity
  • Provide personalised festival information (lineup, map, announcements)
  • Generate anonymised, aggregated crowd density and movement analytics (heatmaps) for festival operators and internal improvement purposes. These datasets contain no personally identifiable information and are processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in improving festival safety and experience.
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs
  • Comply with our legal obligations

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policy:

  • Firebase Authentication — account management (Google Ireland Limited, Ireland)
  • Firebase Realtime Database — live location storage (Google Ireland Limited, Ireland)
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — push notifications (Google Ireland Limited, Ireland)
  • Google Cloud Storage — profile picture storage (Google Ireland Limited, Ireland)
  • Firebase Dynamic Links — deep linking (Google Ireland Limited, Ireland)
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — usage analytics (Google Ireland Limited, Ireland)
  • Google OAuth — sign in with Google (Google Ireland Limited, Ireland)
  • Sign in with Apple — authentication (Apple Inc., United States)
  • Mapbox — festival maps (Mapbox Inc., United States)

Some services are located outside the European Union (Apple and Mapbox in the United States). Data transfers are subject to appropriate safeguards including standard contractual clauses.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all your personal data is permanently removed from our systems, including your profile, location history, and any associated content.

Anonymised heatmap data, which contains no personal identifiers, is not subject to account deletion as it cannot be attributed to any individual.

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

As a user in the European Union, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (Right to Erasure)
  • Restrict or object to processing of your data
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@luuppoint.com. We will respond within one month.

7. Minimum Age

LUUP. is intended for users aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us at support@luuppoint.com and we will delete the account.

8. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. Location data is transmitted over encrypted connections and is visible only to friends you have accepted on LUUP. Heatmap data is anonymised prior to any storage or sharing, ensuring no individual user can be identified.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app. The date at the top of this document reflects the most recent update.

10. Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@luuppoint.com.
